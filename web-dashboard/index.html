Know what you’re measuring

Review days are calendar days between application and issuance for valid issued records. Median is the middle case; P90 shows the long-duration tail. These are elapsed days, not staff effort or legal SLAs.

Historical scope: applications from 2018 onward; the extraction month is excluded. Percentiles recalculate from frequency distributions under your filters, rather than averaging monthly medians. Equal-length preceding periods support comparisons; 100 issues in each period is a stability rule, not statistical significance.

Active workload is a snapshot across all application years. Active issued permits are separate from unissued permits. Unissued age is time elapsed so far, not final review duration.

Quality: 10,730 conflicting-key rows are quarantined, and 25 analytically redundant rows are removed. Terminal and administrative categories are excluded from affected KPIs. Outliers remain; timestamps cannot explain causes.
