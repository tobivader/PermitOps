# Business brief

PermitOps serves a hypothetical Toronto municipal operations manager. The decision is which permit categories merit workflow investigation based on review duration, scale and change. No stakeholder deployment or operational improvements are claimed.

Questions: monthly application/issuance volumes; median/P90 calendar review days; long-duration categories with sufficient cases; comparable recent/prior trends; age and composition of current active workload; investigation priorities.

Scope: current active permits and cleared permits since 2017 from Toronto Open Data. Historical review reporting uses applications from 2018 onward, avoiding the missing pre-2017 cleared population; issuance excludes the incomplete extract month. Active workload uses all application years. Each permit/revision is a record, not a unique building project.

Timestamps include applicant response periods and complexity. They cannot establish staff productivity, causes of delay, or a legal SLA. Issued cohorts leave unresolved applications out of duration calculations, so active unissued age is reported separately.
